About Me

Hi! My name is Adina. I am 21 years old. My name is of Hebrew origin: עֲדִינָא, (pronounced /əˈdiːnə/) and means “delicate” or “gentle.” I’m afraid my priors may disappoint those who subscribe to nominative determinism, but my name does convey something about the way I view the universe[1]: as a delicate balance of interconnected abstractions.

That view also informs my belief that every individual has the capacity to exert meaningful influence on the world. I like to think that the magnitude of said impact is determined by agency; whilst the direction probably has something to do with one’s priors"Bayesian Epistemology", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy and moral commitmentsSome Derivations....

My world-facing self is also likely a product of my priors and the friends I surround myself with.
